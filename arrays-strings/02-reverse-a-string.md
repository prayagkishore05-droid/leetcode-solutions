\## Problem: Reverse a String (Easy)



\*\*Link:\*\* https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/



\### Approach



I used two pointers to reverse the string in place. One pointer starts at the beginning and the other at the end, and their characters are swapped until the pointers meet.



\### Complexity



\- Time: O(n)

\- Space: O(1)



\### Notes



Test Case 1:

Input = "hello"

Expected = "olleh"
