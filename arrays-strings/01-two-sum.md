\## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)



\*\*Link:\*\* https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/



\### Approach



I used a brute-force approach with two nested loops to check every pair of numbers. When a pair adds up to the target, the indices of the matching pair are returned.



\### Complexity



\- Time: O(n²)

\- Space: O(1)



\### Notes



Test Case 1:

nums = {2, 7, 11, 15}, target = 9

Expected: Indices 0, 1



Test Case 2:

nums = {3, 3}, target = 6

Expected: Indices 0, 1



Both test cases were tested locally before submitting to LeetCode. The LeetCode solution was accepted.
